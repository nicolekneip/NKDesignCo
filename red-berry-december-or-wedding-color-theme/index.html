Red Berry December | Wedding Color Theme

If you’re planning a December wedding and dreaming of something elegant, cozy, and seasonally inspired, this red berry winter wedding theme was designed with you in mind. Featuring rich burgundy tones, soft cream textures, and timeless holiday details, these collages bring together stationery, signage, and style inspiration into one cohesive winter wedding vision.

Perfect for DIY December brides, this theme blends romance and simplicity while staying practical and easy to execute.

A Cozy & Elegant December Wedding Aesthetic

December weddings have a charm all their own soft candlelight, layered textures, and rich seasonal colors. This winter wedding theme centers around:

Cream and ivory backdrops

Deep red berry accents

Classic typography

Romantic winter styling

The result is a look that feels festive without being overly themed, making it ideal for couples who want a timeless winter celebration.

Winter Wedding Welcome Sign Inspiration

Your welcome sign is one of the first details guests see, and this red berry winter welcome sign sets the tone beautifully. Displayed on an easel and styled with cream draping, candles, and winter florals, it creates a warm and inviting entrance. This look works especially well for:

Indoor winter 

weddings

Historic venues

Ballrooms and garden rooms

December and holiday weddings

An editable welcome sign template allows DIY brides to personalize names, dates, and locations while keeping the overall aesthetic cohesive.

December Wedding Invitation Flatlay Styling

The invitation collage showcases how stationery can tie your entire wedding vision together. Styled with:

Red ribbon accents

Soft cream fabrics

Winter berries

Jewelry and holiday details

This flatlay demonstrates how a December wedding invitation suite can feel both romantic and elevated. Burgundy and cream invitations are especially popular for winter weddings because they photograph beautifully and complement seasonal décor.

For DIY brides, editable invitation templates make it easy to achieve a professional look without custom design costs.

Winter Bridesmaid Dress Color Inspiration

No December wedding is complete without a stunning bridal party palette. This

 collage features a lineup of bridesmaid dresses in varying shades of red, creating depth while maintaining cohesion.

Red bridesmaid dresses are perfect for:

Winter weddings

Holiday-inspired ceremonies

Classic and romantic wedding styles

Mixing silhouettes while keeping a consistent color palette allows each bridesmaid to feel confident while supporting a unified wedding aesthetic.
