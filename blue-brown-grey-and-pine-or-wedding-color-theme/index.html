Blue, Brown, Grey, & Pine | Wedding Color Theme

Earth-toned weddings continue to be one of the most searched wedding styles for modern couples, and for good reason. Neutral, grounded palettes photograph beautifully, feel timeless, and allow personal details to shine.

The bridesmaid dress collage showcases how this palette comes to life through fashion. Flowing silhouettes in sage green, dusty blue, chocolate brown, and charcoal create visual interest while still feeling cohesive.

The flatlay collage highlights how the color palette translates into the finer details of your wedding day. From brown wedding invitations and sage accessories to soft blue floral accents and pearl jewelry, every element works together effortlessly.

The table décor collage brings the entire palette together in a dramatic yet elegant way. Rich brown draping sets the mood, while ivory linens, sculptural green cake details, and lush blue-and-cream florals soften the look.
