A Little Rookie is on the Way | Baby Shower Theme

Planning a football-themed baby shower? A Little Rookie baby shower is the perfect way to celebrate a new MVP on the way, especially if the parents-to-be are big football fans. From varsity-inspired invitations to game-day baby shower activities, this theme is fun, stylish, and easy to pull together.

Below, I’m sharing football baby shower ideas, printable games, and decor signage to help you host a coordinated and memorable event.

🍼 Little Rookie Baby Shower Theme Overview

A Little Rookie football baby shower blends classic game-day details with soft baby shower elements. This theme works beautifully for:

  • Fall or winter baby showers

  • Co-ed or couples showers

  • Tailgate-style celebrations

  • Neutral or boy baby showers

Popular design elements include:

  • Navy blue, forest green, and gold accents

  • Varsity or graduate-style fonts

  • Football icons and subtle athletic textures

✉️ Football Baby Shower Invitation

Set the tone with a Little Rookie football baby shower invitation featuring a clean, modern varsity design. Editable invitations allow you to customize:

  • Baby’s name

  • Shower date & location

  • Host details

  • Registry information

💡 Tip: Editable Canva invitations are perfect for DIY hosts who want a polished look without custom design costs.
