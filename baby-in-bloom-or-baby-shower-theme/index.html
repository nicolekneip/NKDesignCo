Baby in Bloom | Baby Shower Theme

If you’re planning a baby shower and love soft florals, blush tones, and romantic details, the Baby in Bloom theme is a perfect choice. This popular baby shower style combines garden-inspired florals with elegant pink accents to create a celebration that feels fresh, feminine, and timeless.

This Baby in Bloom inspiration collage was designed to help DIY baby shower organizers easily visualize a cohesive look, from invitations and décor to desserts and table styling.

A Baby in Bloom baby shower is a beautiful way to celebrate new beginnings. With soft florals, romantic pink tones, and cohesive stationery, this theme is both timeless and modern, perfect for DIY planners who want a polished result without the stress.
