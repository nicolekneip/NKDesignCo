Modern Plum & Olive Wedding Stationery for a Chic, Elegant Celebration

Planning a wedding that feels modern, elegant, and effortlessly coordinated? This plum, olive, and cream wedding stationery collection was designed for couples who love timeless style with a contemporary twist.

From your first announcement to your wedding day signage, this cohesive suite helps you create a polished look without the cost of custom design.

A Sophisticated Color Palette for Modern Weddings

Rich plum tones bring depth and romance, while soft cream keeps the look light and timeless. Subtle olive accents add an organic, earthy balance that feels both trendy and classic.

This palette is perfect for:

  • Fall weddings

  • Winter weddings

  • Formal or black-tie celebrations

  • Modern minimalist venues

  • Garden or indoor ceremonies

The result is a refined aesthetic that photographs beautifully and feels intentional from start to finish.

A Fully Coordinated Wedding Stationery Suite

This collection was created to give couples a seamless, designer-quality look across every part of their wedding day.

💌 Save the Date

A modern 5x7 save the date that introduces your wedding style from the very first impression. Elegant typography and a clean layout make it perfect for both print and digital sharing.

💍 Wedding Invitation Suite

Choose between a double-sided 5x7 invitation or a full suite including:

  • Invitation

  • Details Card

  • RSVP Card

Designed to feel minimal, refined, and timeless - while still making a statement.

🪧 Wedding Welcome Sign

Set the tone as guests arrive with a coordinating welcome sign available in multiple sizes. Perfect for easels, entry tables, or ceremony entrances.

🍽 Table Numbers

Modern table numbers (1–30) that tie your reception tables into the overall wedding design. These look beautiful in frames, stands, or minimalist holders.

📖 Wedding Programs

A sleek 4x9 rack card ceremony program that keeps things elegant and easy to read without bulky folded booklets.

Designed for Easy DIY Editing

Every piece in this collection is editable using Canva, making it simple for couples to personalize their wedding stationery without design experience.

You can:

  • Change names and dates

  • Edit ceremony details and wording

  • Adjust table numbers

  • Download files for print or digital use

This gives you the flexibility of custom stationery with the convenience and affordability of a template.

Perfect for Couples Who Want a Cohesive Wedding Look

One of the biggest challenges in wedding planning is making sure everything feels like it belongs together. This bundle solves that by providing matching designs for:

✔ Your announcement
✔ Your invitations
✔ Your ceremony
✔ Your reception signage

No mixing fonts. No clashing styles. Just a beautifully coordinated wedding aesthetic.
